BitMo Labs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

BitMo Labs is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What we access

With the owner's permission, BitMo Labs accesses only the owner's own TikTok account: basic profile info, video upload, and the owner's own video and follower statistics.

What we do not do

BitMo Labs does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from anyone else. It has no other users.

Storage

Access tokens are kept only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings.
